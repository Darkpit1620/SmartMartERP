Important pricing rules

  • Only head-office administrators can import company-wide prices.
  • Retail and wholesale prices must be positive values; blank means do not modify.
  • Cost prices and tax rates must come from verified business records and approved tax treatment.
  • Do not change the SKU column. Unknown or repeated SKUs cause the entire import to be rejected.
  • The importer does not create products, receive stock, adjust stock, or rewrite past receipts.
  • Keep a private backup before large imports. The import is atomic: an invalid CSV will not partially update prices.
