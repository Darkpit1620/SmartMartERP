Run automated end-to-end checks

In VS Code PowerShell, run these commands one at a time. Django uses a disposable test database; they do not create real sales, shipments or expenses.

python manage.py test transaction_checks --noinput

Then verify all installed modules:

python manage.py test --noinput

Finally inspect actual business records:

python manage.py smartmart_audit
A passing test does not prove real payment settlement, compliance with tax invoicing rules or production readiness. Continue to verify a training-branch sale through the actual cashier screen.

Manual workflow checklist

  1. Create a supplier purchase order. Confirm no stock change before delivery.
  2. Receive goods into the training branch. Confirm stock rises by the received amount.
  3. Make a retail cash sale. Confirm a receipt exists and stock falls once.
  4. Make a wholesale sale at the correct price and minimum quantity.
  5. Transfer training stock to another training branch. Verify in-transit and received quantities.
  6. Record an expense. Confirm it remains pending until head-office approval.
  7. Verify a cashier cannot access other branches' managerial pages.
